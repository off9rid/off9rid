### What I'm building

- 🤖 **[Friday](https://github.com/off9rid/friday)**: a done-for-you personal AI assistant for your Mac. Daily briefings, deadlines, memory and voice control. [Live site →](https://meetfriday.vercel.app)
- ✋ **[HALO](https://github.com/off9rid/halo)**: control your Mac with hand gestures through the webcam. On-device MediaPipe, no extra hardware.
- 👏 **[Clap Launcher](https://github.com/off9rid/clap-launcher)**: double-clap and Terminal opens with Claude Code running.
- 💸 **FinLit for Youths**: a redesigned site for a youth financial-literacy program. three.js coin, GSAP scroll.

### How I work

I ship fast with AI in the loop: idea → working product in days, not months. Most of what I build starts as a tool for my own life and turns into something other people want.
